Play the agent committee

At AT&T I built a system where one orchestrator coordinates three specialist agents, and the useful signal was never the final answer — it was where the specialists disagreed. Chess is a clean way to show that, because the objectives genuinely conflict: taking a free pawn can wreck your king.

You are White. Make a move and three agents each search the position with a different objective, then a coordinator reconciles their votes. The engine underneath — move generation, castling, en passant, promotion, alpha–beta search — is written from scratch and verified with perft.
